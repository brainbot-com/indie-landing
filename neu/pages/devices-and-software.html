Indie Suite software

AI Brain

Everything your company knows, one question away. It connects your drives, email, wikis and business systems, and every answer comes with its source.

  • Your sources stay put

    They stay where they are, nothing is copied over, and nobody has to change how they work.

  • Always with the source

    Every answer names the document and the passage. If it isn't in your sources, the AI Brain doesn't claim it.

  • Cleaned up by the Curator

    Old versions, duplicates and contradictions get condensed into clear articles, with links back to the originals.

Indie Suite software

AI Agent Command Center

Your custom apps and AI agents, together in one dashboard. Describe a task, and an agent gets it done with your approved data.

  • Custom apps for your team

    We build apps around how your team works, like a project roadmap or a design simulator. Need another one? Ask the agent and it builds it.

  • An agent for every app

    Each app has its own agent that knows your data. Launch new agents whenever you need them, each set up for its own kind of work.

  • Apps that stay up to date

    Every app an agent builds stays in your dashboard and always shows your latest approved data, so you can come back to it any time.

  • You stay in the loop

    Review and correct the interim results, and approve each step before anything is final.

  • Shared with the right people

    You choose who can use each app and agent. Colleagues only see what they have been given access to.

Indie Suite software

Agent Studio

A protected area for every team, with its own knowledge and its own rules.

  • Areas and knowledge sources

    Each team gets its own area. You decide which documents, databases and file stores it can see, and you can check that at any time.

  • Rules for every area

    Each area sets which sources its agents may use, what they may do, and where a person has to approve.

  • One machine for every team

    HR and product development can work on the same device, and neither sees the other's data.

Platform

Integrations and API

The AI Brain connects to the systems your company already uses, and your apps and agents can use your own tools.

  • Works with what you have

    Shared drives, email and GitLab, connected where they already are. File servers, network drives and more systems, like SharePoint, Teams, Confluence, Notion, SAP, Salesforce and HubSpot, can be connected on request.

  • Your own APIs and MCP servers

    Apps and agents can call your own APIs and remote MCP servers, the same ones in chat and in agents.

  • Also works with the AI you already use

    The AI Brain runs on its own local models. If your team already uses ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot, you can connect them too, so they answer with your company knowledge. Access control decides what each tool can see.

  • Locked to one address

    Each connector is tied to one fixed address that apps and agents cannot change.

  • New skills for your agents

    Teach agents a skill, like summarizing a PDF, and they use it only when it is relevant.

Platform

Orchestration and workflows

Agents take on recurring work and keep running in the background, on a schedule you set.

  • Routines on a schedule

    Give an agent a routine, like a summary of open quotes every Monday, and it runs on that cadence on your own server, even when nobody is logged in.

  • As a chat or an agent

    A routine can answer into its own chat, or run a full agent turn with its workspace and tools.

  • See what is running

    Agents with a routine or a running service move to the Background tab, so you can see at a glance what works on its own.

  • Pause any time

    Pause a routine or stop a service, and the agent moves back to the Foreground.

Platform

Model management

You choose which AI model answers, and you can switch at any time.

  • Pick the default

    Set the model every new chat starts with, like Qwen 3.8 27B running on your own device. Switch it in the chat toolbar whenever you like.

  • Bring your own endpoint

    Connect any OpenAI-compatible API, and it appears as Custom next to your local models. Its key stays in your browser.

  • Add open models

    Add or remove open models on your device whenever you need to.

  • In your administrators' hands

    Only your administrators can install models, and each one can be checked against its published checksum.

Platform

Data and knowledge

Your team and your agents work from one knowledge base, kept in one place on your device.

  • One knowledge base, with guardrails

    Every connected source feeds the AI Brain. Your team and your agents each see only the knowledge they have been given access to.

  • Private until shared

    What someone creates stays private until they choose to share it with the team.

  • Simple to back up

    All data sits in one central area on your device, so it fits into the backup routine you already have.

Platform

Security and governance

The system itself enforces who sees what, and what each agent may do.

  • Access set per agent

    For every wiki, you choose which agents can read it and which one maintains it. Only an admin can change who maintains the shared wiki.

  • Personal data filtered out

    A privacy filter can replace emails, phone numbers and secrets with placeholders before a request reaches the model.

  • A sealed space for every agent

    Each app and agent runs in its own closed space, with no internet unless you allow it.

  • Access per person

    Everyone has their own account, and each team only sees the areas it has been given. Signing in with your company login is available as an add-on.

  • Kept to yourself

    The device sends nothing back to us, and your data is never used to train AI.

Frequently asked
questions.

Is this an alternative to ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot?

Yes, for companies that want to keep their data in-house. Your team asks questions in plain language, like with ChatGPT or Copilot, but the AI runs on-premise on your own device, works with your company knowledge, and is set up to meet GDPR requirements.

What is on-premise AI?

On-premise AI runs on hardware in your own building instead of a provider's cloud. With indie.solutions, that is one of four devices, from the compact indie.box to the indie.rack, with the AI Brain, AI Agent Command Center and Agent Studio preinstalled.

Which device should we start with?

Most teams start with the Essential package and the indie.box. If people often wait for answers while they work, for example when coding, the indie.workstation is the better fit. For 100 users or more, the indie.rack is the right choice.

Is the software the same on every device?

Yes. Every device runs the same software, so your apps and agents keep working when you add or change devices.

Is the software included in the price?

Yes. The AI Brain, AI Agent Command Center and Agent Studio come preinstalled on every device, and each package includes 12 months of the AI Brain and of updates, support and device replacement. After that, you choose monthly plans, priced per device for any number of users.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. The devices can run fully offline.

Who can access our data?

Only the people you give access to. Your data stays on your device and is never used to train AI models.

Can we use our own AI models?

Yes. Your administrators can add open models next to the preinstalled ones, or connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Does it connect to the systems we already use?

Yes. The AI Brain connects to shared drives, email and code repositories such as GitLab and GitHub, without moving your data. File servers, network drives and other systems, such as SharePoint, Confluence or Salesforce, can be connected on request.

How do updates work?

Updates, security patches and tested model updates are part of every plan for updates, support and device replacement, and the first 12 months are included in your package. Your administrators start each update from the admin dashboard, so nothing changes without their approval.

Can we add more devices later?

Yes. Add an indie.booster to an indie.box, add workstations, or move to a rack. What you have stays part of the system.
