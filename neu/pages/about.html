Privacy should never be an add-on.

brainbot develops AI and software systems for environments where trust, control and auditability are essential.

Since 2014, brainbot has built privacy infrastructure for critical systems: sensitive business processes, private AI infrastructure, cryptographic protocols and digital elections.
