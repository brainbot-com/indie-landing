Add speed
to your indie.box.

The indie.booster adds an NVIDIA RTX 5090 to an indie.box, without replacing it. The box keeps its 128 GB of memory for large models, and the booster adds fast answers. Together they are the only setup with both.

  • Choose it when

    You want large models and fast answers in one setup, or your indie.box should become faster.

  • Good to know

    The booster works only with an indie.box. It does not run on its own or with the indie.workstation.

Frequently asked
questions.

Do we need an indie.box?

Yes. The booster only works together with an indie.box.

Can we add it later?

Yes, at any time, also to a box that is already in use.

Does it work with the indie.workstation?

No. The workstation already has its own built-in GPU.

What changes in daily use?

Very little. Access, users and updates stay the same. The booster takes over the requests that need speed.
