Frequently asked
questions.

Need more speed later?

Add an indie.booster to the box at any time. For interactive work all day, the indie.workstation is the better fit.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes. It can run fully offline.

Who can access our data?

Only your own administrators. The box sends no telemetry.

Can we use our own AI models?

Yes. You can add your own models next to the preinstalled ones, and remove them again.

Do we need our own IT staff?

Your team uses it in the browser. Some technical know-how helps for the network setup and company login (SSO).

How do people connect?

In the browser, on desktop or mobile, with local accounts or your company login (SSO or LDAP).

How do backups work?

All data sits in one central data area, which you include in your existing backup routine.

What if the hardware has a defect?

The statutory warranty applies, one year for businesses.
