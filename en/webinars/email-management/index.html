Email management with local AI agents.

Nov19

Thursday, November 19, 2026 · 4:00 PM CET · Online

60 minutes · Google Meet · Free

For managing directors, office managers and anyone whose working day disappears into their inbox. The agents work with your AI Brain, so every reply draws on what your company already knows.

TO CHECK: Confirm the date and time of this webinar.

What you will get out of it

  • See agents sort your inbox

    Watch a local agent read incoming email, sort it and flag what needs your attention.

  • See the AI Brain behind every reply

    Watch an agent look up the answer in your AI Brain, from past emails, documents and quotes, and draft a reply with the source attached. It waits for your approval before anything is sent.

  • Understand how it stays private

    The agents run on a device in your own office with read-only access by default, so your email is never sent to a cloud AI provider.

  • Get your questions answered

    Bring your own questions. There is time for an open Q&A at the end.
