Email management with local AI agents.

Date coming soon · Online

60 minutes · Google Meet · Free

For managing directors, office managers and anyone whose working day disappears into their inbox. The agents work with your AI Brain, so every reply draws on what your company already knows.

What your agent does for you

  • Only read what matters

    The agent sorts your inbox overnight and, in the morning, shows you only what really needs your decision.

  • Stay on top of long emails

    You know right away what the sender wants and by when, even if they wrote three pages.

  • Never forget a thing

    The agent keeps track of every open request and every commitment, and reminds you before anyone has to follow up.

  • Replies with the full picture

    The agent knows your quotes, prices and agreements and puts the finished reply in your drafts. You review it and click send.

Experience AI that belongs to you, live

No slides. A real inbox, live.

  • How it works without the cloud

    The AI runs on a device in your office, connected directly to your inbox. We show you what is inside, so you can explain it yourself afterward.

  • Waiting time you never notice

    Local AI takes longer than the cloud. That is why the agent works in the background, and when you open your inbox, everything is ready.

  • Why no one else reads along

    Your emails never leave the building. No cloud provider and no AI company ever gets to see them.
