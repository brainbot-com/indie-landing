Your data never leaves
your control.

The AI Brain runs on a server in your office, or in a sealed cloud that not even we can see into.

  • Sealed environment

    Each agent works in a closed-off space that controls what it can reach, with no internet unless you allow it.

  • Read-only by default

    The agent makes changes only through actions you switch on.

  • Access for the right people

    Employees can keep knowledge in the AI Brain private or share it with the team, so each person sees only what they should.

  • Locked apps stay locked

    You decide for each app whether the agent may change it or only use it.

  • Full audit trail

    Every question and action is logged, so you can show an auditor what happened.

  • Never used for training

    Your designs, IP and everything your team asks or stores are never used to train AI.

  • GDPR compliant

    On your own server, your data stays in your building and you remain the data controller.

  • Built for IP-sensitive work

    Designs, code and test data never reach an outside AI.

  • DPA included

    We sign a data processing agreement before setup.

Where it can run

The protections above apply to all three.

On a server in your office

We set up a server from indie.box in your office. It runs open AI models locally, so nothing goes to the cloud.

  • Set up by us, in your office
  • Your data never leaves your building
  • Works without an internet connection

In your own cloud account

We set it up in the cloud you already use.

  • Runs under your keys and your access rules
  • Uses the cloud provider you already have
  • No hardware to install in your office

In a sealed cloud (TEE)

The AI runs in an encrypted cloud service. We set up your account and connect it to your AI Brain.

  • No hardware to buy or install
  • Pay only for what you use
  • Encrypted, so not even we or the cloud provider can see your data

Frequently asked
questions.

Where is our data stored and processed?

With a device from indie.solutions, your data is stored and processed on that device in your office and does not leave your building. If you choose the sealed cloud (TEE) instead, your data stays encrypted even while it is being processed, so neither we nor the cloud provider can see it.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Nothing your team asks or stores is used to train AI models, by us or by anyone else.

Is it GDPR compliant?

Yes. On your own device, your data stays in your building and you remain the data controller. Where we provide support, we sign a data processing agreement with you before setup.

Can we control which information each employee can see?

Yes. You decide which knowledge sources each team can access, and employees can keep their own content private or share it with their team. Everyone sees only what they have been given access to.

Can AI agents change our data?

Only if you allow it. By default, agents can only read. They make changes only through actions you switch on, you decide for each app whether an agent may edit it, and every action is logged.

Which AI models does it use?

Open AI models such as Qwen, GPT-OSS and Llama. On your own device they run locally, so nothing is sent to an outside AI provider. Your administrators can add further models at any time.

Does it connect to the systems we already use?

Yes. The AI Brain connects to your shared drives, email and code repositories such as GitLab and GitHub, with read-only access, and your data stays where it is. File servers, network drives and other systems, such as SharePoint, Confluence or Salesforce, can be connected on request.

We already use ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot. Does the AI Brain work with them?

Yes. The AI Brain runs on its own local models, and your team can also connect the AI tools they already use, so those answer with your company knowledge too. You decide which tools are connected and what each one can see. Anything those tools receive is processed by their provider, so sensitive questions are best asked in the AI Brain itself.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes, on your own device. The AI runs locally in your office and needs no internet connection. The sealed cloud (TEE) requires an internet connection.

What hardware do we need?

Every package includes the device, delivered to your office with the software preinstalled. All you need is a place for it and a connection to your network. The sealed cloud (TEE) needs no hardware at all.

Do we need our own IT staff to operate it?

No. We set up the device, connect your first systems and train your team, and our support plans keep it running and up to date. Basic IT knowledge helps with your network settings and, if you add it, the company login.

How long does setup take?

Usually about 14 days until your AI Brain is up and running. Setup ends with a two-day workshop with your team, in which we connect your first systems and build your first custom apps.

Who builds the apps?

With the setup workshop, we build two custom apps with your team. After that, your team can ask the agent to build new apps, and we can build more for you on request.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. Request a trial, and we'll send you access to a web portal where your team can try the AI Brain before you decide on a device.

What happens when our team grows?

Prices are per device, so adding colleagues does not change the price. If your company knowledge grows, you can move to a larger AI Brain plan, and if you need more speed, an indie.booster adds power to your indie.box. With the sealed cloud, usage fees grow with how much you use it.

Can the apps use our company branding?

Yes. Your apps can show your company name and logo, as in the Lightforce example in the videos on this page.

What happens to our apps and data if we end the contract?

They remain yours. The apps, their settings and your data belong to you, also after the contract ends.

What does it cost?

The starter bundle costs €7,999, net plus VAT. It includes the indie.box, a two-day setup workshop, two custom apps and 12 months of Standard support. You can also put together your own setup at regular prices. Prices are per device, for any number of users.
